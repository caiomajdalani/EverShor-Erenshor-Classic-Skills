## Necromancer Ability Audio

`Resources/Audio/Necromancer/*.wav` are edited mono derivatives of two CC0
sources, mapped so that every Necromancer ability names its own cast and
resolve layer. Per-file provenance is recorded in
`Resources/Audio/Necromancer/LICENSES.md`.

- Fourteen files derive from
  [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx) by
  rubberduck, offered under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Five files derive from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) by
  Joseph SARDIN, offered under CC0 1.0 / WTFPL / public domain.

Edits in both cases: decoded to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM, trimmed to the useful
transient, faded, and loudness-normalised to -14 LUFS with a -1 dBTP ceiling.
Runtime pitch, level, and layering are authored by the addon.
